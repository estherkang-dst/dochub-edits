# Meta

## Activate on Meta

Dstillery delivers your audiences to Meta through **LiveRamp**, our activation partner. Before the first delivery, your team gives LiveRamp reporting access to your Meta ad account. You do this once per ad account, and we handle delivery from there.

{% columns %}
{% column %}
#### Set up once

Give LiveRamp access to each Meta ad account where audiences will run.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
#### Activate with confidence

We handle delivery. You target the delivered audiences in your ad sets.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

### From setup to launch

1. **You connect LiveRamp.** Add LiveRamp as a partner on your ad account, using the steps below.
2. **We set up delivery.** Dstillery connects delivery to your ad account and sends the audiences you approved.
3. **Audiences arrive.** They're shared into your ad account. We'll confirm the go-live date when we send them.
4. **You launch.** Target the audiences in your ad sets. We invoice data usage separately from Meta.

### Before you start

#### Who completes setup

Someone with full control of the Meta business portfolio that owns the ad account.

#### What we need from you

* **Complete the Social End User Acknowledgment** at [dstillery.com/social-end-user-acknowledgment](https://dstillery.com/social-end-user-acknowledgment). It collects your company name, primary and billing contacts, and billing address.
* **Send your Dstillery contact your numeric Ad Account ID(s)**, the one detail the form doesn't collect. In Meta: **Business settings › Accounts › Ad accounts**. This is not your Business portfolio ID.

{% hint style="info" %}
**LiveRamp's Meta Business ID: 705664196188477.** You'll enter this in step 4 below.
{% endhint %}

### Add LiveRamp as a partner in Meta

Done once in Meta Business Suite, by someone with full control of the portfolio that owns the ad account.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
In Meta Business Suite, click **Settings**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Under Users, click **Partners**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Click **+ Add**, then select **Give a partner access to your assets**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Enter LiveRamp's Business ID **705664196188477** and click **Next**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Under asset types, select **Ad accounts**, then tick every ad account Dstillery audiences may be sent to, now or later. Adding an ad account later means repeating these steps.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Turn on the right access for the ad accounts you selected:

| Access                     |          | What it's for                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **View performance**       | Required | Lets LiveRamp pull the monthly usage report that data usage is billed from.                                                                               |
| **Manage campaigns (ads)** | Optional | Only if you also send your own first-party data through LiveRamp's Direct integration. Then also accept Meta's Custom Audience Terms for that ad account. |
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Click **Assign assets**, then **Done**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
If LiveRamp sends an audience sharing request to your Business Manager, accept it. That's it: we'll take it from here.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Screens look different?** These steps follow LiveRamp's guide, updated Oct 15, 2026. Meta changes its Business Suite screens from time to time, so if something doesn't match or setup fails, follow LiveRamp's current instructions: [Adding LiveRamp as a Partner in Facebook](https://docs.liveramp.com/connect/en/adding-liveramp-as-a-partner-in-facebook.html). LiveRamp also has an [interactive walkthrough](https://liveramp.storylane.io/share/p6gqwozpd6ue).

### After your audiences arrive

#### Finding and using your audiences

* We'll send the exact audience names once they're delivered. Select them in your ad set's targeting.
* Use them for targeting. Meta Lookalikes aren't supported for audiences delivered this way.
* Meta shows audience size as an estimated range, not an exact count.
* "Audience too small" means it hasn't reached Meta's minimum size yet. Give it a few days, then let us know.

#### How billing works

* Meta can't bill third-party data fees, so Dstillery invoices you directly, separate from your Meta bill.
* We receive your Meta usage through LiveRamp's reporting, one month behind. Your first invoice arrives the month after your audiences go live, and each invoice covers the previous month.
* Keep LiveRamp's View performance access on until your campaigns end and the last month is reported.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Best practice: budget for Dstillery data outside your Meta budget.** Our fees won't appear in Meta, so set aside budget for our invoice based on your planned spend with our audiences and the rate in your agreement. Track which campaigns and ad sets use them, so invoices are easy to check.
{% endhint %}

### If audiences don't appear

Check that:

* LiveRamp is a partner on the right ad account
* View performance is turned on
* You sent us the Ad Account ID, not the Business portfolio ID
* Any sharing request from LiveRamp was accepted
* Meta's screens match the steps above. If they don't, follow [LiveRamp's current setup guide](https://docs.liveramp.com/connect/en/adding-liveramp-as-a-partner-in-facebook.html)
