# Retail purchase intent audiences

### What it is

Retail Purchase Intent Audiences are specialized Custom Built Audiences seeded with purchase-intent signals. They help you reach shoppers who are actively in-market, rather than people who only match a demographic profile.

Dstillery identifies devices showing strong purchase intent, then finds similar shoppers at scale across programmatic channels.

Unlike on-site retail media, which is limited to retailer environments, these audiences extend reach across the open web. Engage high-intent shoppers wherever they spend time.

Use DS-1 to choose product seeds and build a purchase intent audience. See [build-a-purchase-intent-audience.md](../../using-ds-1/agents/build-a-purchase-intent-audience.md "mention").

Prefer a managed approach? Your Dstillery Client Services team can build this for you.

### How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Capture purchase signals

Dstillery ingests retail purchase intent signals from partner data and behavioral activity, such as product-page visits.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Score purchase likelihood

Multimodal AI scores 400M+ devices by likelihood to purchase.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Deliver and refresh

A predictive audience is delivered to your DSP within 24 hours. The model refreshes every 24 hours, so targeting reflects current shopping intent.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Activate

One model powers every activation method: user segments, contextual, curated deals, and custom bidding algorithms.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
