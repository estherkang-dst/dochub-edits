# Pre-built audiences

### What it is

Pre-built Audiences give you instant access to precise, ready-to-activate targeting without custom build or setup time. With 10,000+ audiences powered by event-level behavioral, demographic, search, and exclusive partner data, there is a segment for virtually every campaign goal.

<a href="https://audiences.dstillery.com" class="button primary" data-icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">Browse audiences</a>

Use DS-1 to search the catalog in plain language and syndicate audiences to your DSP. See [find-prebuilt-audiences.md](../../ds-1-guide/agents/find-prebuilt-audiences.md "mention").
