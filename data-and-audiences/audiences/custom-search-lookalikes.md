# Custom search lookalikes

### What it is

Custom Search Lookalikes are a specialized type of Custom Built Audience. They are seeded with search intent signals from an opted-in panel.

Dstillery uses opted-in search panel data and ID-free technology. It identifies people searching for your brand's keywords and finds others with the same behaviors across the open web, CTV, and beyond. The result is search-quality intent at programmatic scale, without cookies or deterministic IDs.

### Why it's different

{% columns %}
{% column %}
#### Before

Search intent is powerful but siloed. It lives in Google or Bing and cannot activate across the open web, CTV, or other programmatic environments.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
#### After

Dstillery captures search signals from an opted-in panel. Multimodal AI finds matching search behaviors across the programmatic landscape, so your keywords work harder everywhere.

### How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Choose keywords

Target keywords can be selected, or Dstillery can recommend them based on campaign goals.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Analyze search behavior

Multimodal AI analyzes behavior from an opted-in, consented search panel of approximately two million users.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Map intent at scale

Those patterns map across 400M+ devices to find people exhibiting the same intent signals.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Deliver and refresh

A custom predictive model is built and delivered to your DSP within 24 hours. The model refreshes every 24 hours, targeting the most current in-market signals.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
