# Custom patient targeting

### What it is

Custom Patient Targeting enables marketers to reach the right patients in a privacy-safe, compliant way. It uses medical claims data and patient search behavior to build a custom, condition-specific predictive model.

Rather than relying on broad health segments, Dstillery identifies relevant impressions through confirmed diagnoses and patient-journey search behavior. The model uses 40+ billion medical and pharmacy claims covering 330+ million patient lives and 98% of payer data. This depth supports precise, condition-specific models across virtually any indication.
