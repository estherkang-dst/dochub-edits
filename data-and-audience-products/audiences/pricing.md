# Pricing

{% hint style="success" %}
Dstillery audiences are priced as a percentage of media spend, with a CPM cap. There is no setup fee or minimum commitment. You pay for an audience only when your campaign is live.
{% endhint %}

### Pricing by audience type

Choose the audience that fits your campaign. Pricing scales with media spend, up to the listed CPM cap.

| Audience                             | Pricing                                              |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Custom AI**                        | 30% of media spend, capped at $2.50 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **Custom Built**                     | 30% of media spend, capped at $2.50 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **Custom Search Lookalikes**         | 30% of media spend, capped at $2.50 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **Retail Purchase Intent**           | 30% of media spend, capped at $2.50 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **Pre-built**                        | 25% of media spend, capped at $0.99 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **CTV-Optimized**                    | 30% of media spend, capped at $2.50 CPM or $0.90 CPM |
| **Custom Patient Targeting**         | 35% of media spend or $2.50 CPM                      |
| **Custom Patient Targeting for CTV** | 10% of media spend or $2.50 CPM                      |

Audiences refresh every 24 hours at no extra cost. Pricing stays the same whether your campaign runs for a week or a quarter.
