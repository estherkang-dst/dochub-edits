# Custom AI audiences

### What it is

Custom AI Audiences solve the problem of underutilized first-party data. Instead of using your CRM list or pixel data for retargeting only, Dstillery's multimodal AI takes those signals and builds a predictive model that finds your next best customers across the entire programmatic landscape.

The model learns from your actual customers, not generic demographic proxies. It scores 400M+ devices daily to find people who look and behave like them.

### Why it's different

{% columns %}
{% column %}
#### Before

Your first-party data, whether it is a CRM list, pixel, or conversion log, is typically limited to retargeting or basic lookalike modeling. These approaches rely on single signals and do not scale well for finding new customers.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
#### After

Dstillery combines all of your data into one predictive model. It uses real behavioral patterns to find new customers at scale, not just people who resemble them on paper.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

### How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Prepare the seed

First-party seed data can include site visitors, conversion pixels, or campaign logs. [create-a-pixel.md](../../using-ds-1/agents/create-a-pixel.md "mention") in DS-1 to collect site-visitor and conversion signals. Work with Dstillery Client Services for managed setup.

CRM lists require Client Services for matching and ingestion. Dstillery requires at least 1,000 seeds to build a predictive model.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Analyze digital journeys

Dstillery's multimodal AI analyzes full digital journey patterns across 400M+ devices.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Receive and refresh

A custom predictive model is built and delivered to your DSP within 24 hours. The model refreshes every 24 hours, so your audience never goes stale.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Activate everywhere

One model powers every activation method, from user segments to custom bidding algorithms.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Key benefits

{% hint style="success" %}
* Built from your own data, so the model reflects your actual customers, not generic segments.
* Activatable as ID-based or cookieless via ID-free technology, flexible across all environments.
* Uses event-level behavioral modeling across customers' full digital journey, not basic overlap matching.
{% endhint %}
