# Glossary

#### 24-Hour Scoring

Dstillery refreshes and re-scores audiences every 24 hours to keep models current.

#### Activation

Activation makes an audience available through programmatic channels. Choose the channel that fits your campaign. [activation](../using-ds-1/get-started/activation/ "mention")

#### Agent

An agent is a DS-1 capability that finds, builds, analyzes, or optimizes audiences from your inputs. Unlike a workflow, an agent performs the audience task. [agents](../using-ds-1/agents/ "mention")

#### Audience Brief

An audience brief is an editable strategy generated from your campaign inputs. It recommends Dstillery products and build inputs before you build an audience. [build-or-refine-an-audience-brief.md](../using-ds-1/agents/build-or-refine-an-audience-brief.md "mention")

#### Audience Seed

The data or signals used to train an audience model, such as CRM lists or conversion pixels.

#### Compound Audience

A compound audience combines segments with `AND` or `OR` logic. You can also exclude selected segments. [build-compound-audience.md](../using-ds-1/agents/build-compound-audience.md "mention")

#### Consented Panel Data

About two million opted-in panel users provide cross-site journeys that train intent models. This data trains models and does not directly target individuals. [dstillery-data-and-ds-1-how-they-fit-together.md](../data-and-audience-products/data-and-audiences/dstillery-data-and-ds-1-how-they-fit-together.md "mention")

#### Conversion

A conversion is an outcome measured by a pixel, such as a purchase or form submission. It powers predictive lift analysis. [analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md](../using-ds-1/agents/analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md "mention")

#### CTV

CTV means connected TV. Dstillery uses web browsing and search intent to help reach relevant CTV viewers. [ctv-optimized-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/ctv-optimized-audiences.md "mention")

#### CTV-Optimized Audience

A CTV-Optimized Audience applies web browsing and search behavior to CTV targeting. It uses the DSP's device graph in cookieless CTV environments. [ctv-optimized-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/ctv-optimized-audiences.md "mention")

#### Custom AI Audience

A Custom AI Audience uses first-party signals, such as conversion pixels, site visitors, or CRM lists. It learns behavioral patterns beyond basic lookalike modeling to find similar prospects. [custom-ai-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/custom-ai-audiences.md "mention")

#### Custom Built Audience

A Custom Built Audience uses signals such as websites, keywords, products, or Pre-Built Audiences. Use it when you lack first-party data but know your target's behaviors. [custom-built-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/custom-built-audiences.md "mention")

#### Custom Patient Targeting

Custom Patient Targeting builds condition-specific predictive audiences from medical and pharmacy claims and patient search behavior. It reaches relevant patients without relying on PII. [custom-patient-targeting.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/custom-patient-targeting.md "mention")

#### Custom Search Lookalike

A Custom Search Lookalike is a Custom Built Audience seeded with opted-in search-panel signals. It extends keyword intent across programmatic channels without cookies or deterministic IDs. [custom-search-lookalikes.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/custom-search-lookalikes.md "mention")

#### Device Graph

\[NEEDS REVIEW] A DSP device graph supports applying web and search-based intent to CTV inventory. The docs do not define its identity-resolution mechanics. [ctv-optimized-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/ctv-optimized-audiences.md "mention")

#### Domain-Seeded Audience

A Domain-Seeded Audience models visitors to selected websites or domains. DS-1 can group seeds into separate personas before building. [build-a-domain-seeded-audience.md](../using-ds-1/agents/build-a-domain-seeded-audience.md "mention")

#### DSP

\[NEEDS REVIEW] A DSP is a destination platform where audiences are activated or syndicated. The docs do not define the acronym or platform role. [programmatic-dsps-ssps.md](../using-ds-1/get-started/activation/programmatic-dsps-ssps.md "mention")

#### Exclusion

An exclusion removes people in specified segments from a compound audience. [build-compound-audience.md](../using-ds-1/agents/build-compound-audience.md "mention")

#### First-Party Data

Data collected directly from customer interactions, such as CRM lists, site visits, or conversions.

#### ID-Based

Targeting tied to a specific device or user identifier. It allows audiences to be activated and measured at the individual level.

#### ID-Free

Targeting built from anonymous, aggregated behavioral signals rather than device or user identifiers. It performs without cookies or IDs while meeting the same performance benchmarks as ID-based tactics.

#### Insights

Insights analyzes a topic, brand, domain, or eligible pixel audience. It shows who the audience is, what predicts them, and where to reach them. [insights.md](../using-ds-1/insights.md "mention")

#### LAL

LAL is an abbreviation for Lookalike Audience.

#### LiveRamp Data Marketplace

DS-1 lets you search third-party segments from LiveRamp marketplace providers. Compare options, subscribe, and distribute a selected segment to a platform seat. [liveramp-data-marketplace.md](../using-ds-1/agents/liveramp-data-marketplace.md "mention")

#### Lookalike Audience

\[NEEDS REVIEW] The docs use this as a generic modeling term and distinguish Custom AI Audiences from basic lookalike modeling. They do not define a standalone Lookalike Audience product.

#### Multimodal AI

Multimodal AI maps behavioral, language, and partner signals into a shared intelligence layer. It evaluates keywords, web visits, CTV habits, and purchase data together to identify in-market intent. [how-our-multimodal-ai-works.md](../data-and-audience-products/data-and-audiences/how-our-multimodal-ai-works.md "mention")

#### Partnership Audiences

Partnership Audiences are ready-to-activate segments from NIQ, Emporia, ScreenEngine, and Resonate. Dstillery combines partner data with browsing, search-intent, and CTV signals in predictive intent models. [partnership-audience-search.md](../using-ds-1/agents/partnership-audience-search.md "mention")

#### Persona

\[NEEDS REVIEW] Personas can group domain seeds or describe named clusters in lift results. The docs do not define how DS-1 creates or qualifies a persona.

#### Pixel

A pixel is a tracking tag placed on selected pages. It records visits and supplies first-party data for Custom AI Audiences and predictive lift analysis. [create-a-pixel.md](../using-ds-1/agents/create-a-pixel.md "mention")

#### Pre-Built Audience

A Pre-Built Audience is a ready-to-activate segment from behavioral, demographic, search, or partner data. It requires no custom build. [pre-built-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/pre-built-audiences.md "mention")

#### Predictive Lift Score

Predictive Lift Score is another name for Segrank. It measures how much more likely an audience is to convert against a selected pixel than random targeting. [analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md](../using-ds-1/agents/analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md "mention")

#### Project

A Project is a dedicated workspace for a client's or campaign's files, audiences, instructions, and chat history. It keeps that work separate from other projects. [projects.md](../using-ds-1/projects.md "mention")

#### Proxy Signals

Behavioral inputs that represent the people a campaign aims to reach, such as content consumption or URL visits.

#### Random Baseline

A Random Baseline is the random-targeting benchmark used to calculate predicted conversion lift against a pixel. [analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md](../using-ds-1/agents/analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md "mention")

#### Retail Purchase Intent Audience

A Retail Purchase Intent Audience is a Custom Built Audience seeded with retail purchase-intent signals. It identifies shoppers likely to purchase across programmatic channels. [retail-purchase-intent-audiences.md](../data-and-audience-products/audiences/retail-purchase-intent-audiences.md "mention")

#### Segrank

Segrank is also called a predictive lift score. It measures how much more likely an audience is to convert against a selected pixel than random targeting. [analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md](../using-ds-1/agents/analyze-predictive-lift-scores.md "mention")

#### SSP

\[NEEDS REVIEW] An SSP is a destination platform where audiences are activated or syndicated. The docs do not distinguish SSPs from DSPs. [programmatic-dsps-ssps.md](../using-ds-1/get-started/activation/programmatic-dsps-ssps.md "mention")

#### Syndication

\[NEEDS REVIEW] Syndication sends selected audiences or segments to a marketer, destination, or platform for use. The docs do not explain the delivery mechanism.

#### Third-Party Segments

\[NEEDS REVIEW] These are provider-supplied segments, including marketplace segments and catalog audiences. The docs do not establish whether those categories always have the same scope.

#### Workflow

\[NEEDS REVIEW] A workflow is a saved sequence of steps for a repeated action. Unlike an agent, it defines the sequence rather than performing the audience task; the docs do not otherwise define workflows.
